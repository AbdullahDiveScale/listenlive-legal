Listen Live privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Listen Live processes sound on your phone in real time. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Who we are

Listen Live is published by DiveScale. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Microphone audio

  • Audio from the microphone is processed in memory, a few milliseconds at a time, and played straight to your headphones.
  • Audio is never recorded, saved to storage, transcribed or analysed for content.
  • Audio is never transmitted. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data off your device.
  • The microphone is only active while a session is running, shown by the ongoing "Listen Live is active" notification and Android's microphone indicator.

Permissions we use

  • Microphone: to capture the sound around you during a session.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to detect when your earbuds connect or disconnect, so the app can pause instead of playing through the loudspeaker. The app does not scan for or pair with devices.
  • Notifications: to show the session notification with Mute and Stop buttons.
  • Foreground service: to keep listening while the screen is off.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences (volume, equalizer, presets, routing mode and similar) are saved in the app's private storage so they are there next time. They never leave your phone and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal profiles.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone. There is no data to share.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and the date at the top will change.
